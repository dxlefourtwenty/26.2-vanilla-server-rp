Foods Plus for Minecraft 26.2

The recipes, item textures, and original food behavior were adapted from
Klei's Matcha Flavoured 1.01 for Minecraft 26.2.

Matcha Flavoured replaces hunger restoration with short, high-amplifier
Regeneration effects. Foods Plus removes only those short healing bursts and
converts the displayed healing amount into conventional hunger points, capped
at 10. Saturation is 0.6 per hunger point. Other effects are preserved.

All custom foods use a poisonous potato carrier with their own item model and
components. Recipes use ordinary survival ingredients and do not consume
Matcha's repurposed spawn eggs or other hidden carrier items.

The original Matcha Flavoured credits are included in this archive.
